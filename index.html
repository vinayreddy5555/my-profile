SDLC Methodologies

I will write a paragraph for each methodology of SDLC. My suggestions and reasons are:

  • Agile is a flexible and iterative approach to software development and project management. It focuses on collaboration, adaptability, and delivering value to customers through small, incremental steps instead of completing an entire project at once. This approach allows teams to quickly respond to changes in requirements, ensuring that the final product meets customer needs.
    How Agile Works:
    • Iterative Development: Work is broken into small cycles called iterations. Each iteration delivers a working part of the software.
    • Frequent Feedback: After each iteration, feedback is collected from stakeholders to refine the product.
    • Prioritized Backlog: Tasks and features are listed in a backlog and prioritized to ensure the most important work is done first.
    • Regular Meetings: Teams hold daily stand-ups and sprint reviews to stay aligned and address roadblocks.
  • SPIRAL: The Spiral model enables project teams to create a highly customized product. It consists of four phases—planning, risk analysis, engineering, and evaluation—repeatedly iterated until the project is complete. The main distinction between the Spiral model and other Software Development Life Cycle (SDLC) methodologies is its emphasis on risk analysis. With each iteration, the model focuses on identifying and mitigating potential risks. Additionally, it prioritizes customer feedback, and because prototypes are built incrementally, cost estimation becomes more manageable.
  • Iterative: In the Iterative model, development teams construct software individually, identifying further requirements as they progress. Each new phase results in a more refined software version under development. The Iterative approach allows developers and testers to detect functional or design flaws early on and easily adapt to the changing needs of the client. Like the Spiral model, the Iterative approach is well-suited for larger projects and requires more management and oversight to be effective.
